Looking for mods for The Godfather 2? gf2mod adds a bigger crew, more made men in your family tree, recruitable soldiers and a
minimap that shows who is around you. Install and configure every mod from one window, then press **PLAY**. No game file is ever
modified.

> This repository presents the project. It contains documentation and images only: downloads are on
> [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/gf2mod), where new versions arrive first.

## The Godfather II mods

| Mod | What it does | Version |
|---|---|---|
| [Bigger Crew](docs/mods/bigger-crew.md) | Take up to 127 crew members with you instead of 3. | 1.0.1 |
| [Recruitable Soldiers](docs/mods/recruitable-soldiers.md) | Recruit your venue guards. They stay with you and get a random weapon. | 1.0.1 |
| [Bigger Family Tree](docs/mods/bigger-family-tree.md) | Grow your family from 7 to 10 made men. | 1.0.1 |
| [Minimap Icons](docs/mods/minimap-icons.md) | See your crew, rivals, police and your last car on the radar. | 1.0.0 |

All of them need the [gf2mod manager](docs/manager.md), version 1.1.0.

## Why use gf2mod

- **One window:** turn mods and their individual parts on and off, and change their options, without editing files.
- **Safe to try:** nothing in the game folder is replaced, and removing gf2mod takes the game back to normal.
- **Checked before use:** a package made for another game version, or a damaged one, is refused with a clear message.
- **Conflict control:** if two mods try to change the same part of the game, the second change is refused and logged.
- **ASI plugins included:** gf2mod also loads 32-bit `.asi` plugins, so you don't need a separate loader.
- **Windowed mode:** with the game's brightness applied correctly.

## How to install mods for The Godfather II

1. Check that your game is **version 1.0.789**, the latest official patch.
2. Download gf2mod and the mods you want from [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/gf2mod).
3. Follow the [installation guide](docs/getting-started.md).
